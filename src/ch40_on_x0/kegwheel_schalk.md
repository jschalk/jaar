# KegWheel Model of Power

**Date:** 2026-06-09  
**Author:** Emmanuel Schalk

> How to demand change.

---

Reality is infinitely complex. Any model of reality will be wrong. But some models are better than others. 


The KegWheel is a model of understanding power and predicting how to affect change. The easiest introduction to the concepts of KegWheel is from the writings of Kamil Kazani. Quickest primer on how power is structured: [Mechanics of Revolution](mechanics_of_revolution_kazani.md)  

As with any grand theory the hardest thing is deciding where to start.

<div align="center">

### Start with Family
</div>

The primary unit of history is the FamilyUnit. Ronald Syne wrote ""In all ages, whatever the form and name of government, be it monarchy, republic, or democracy, an oligarchy lurks behind the façade."[^1] There are powerful families that decide how things are done. Families will mostly have one dominate personality but over time that can change and its better to think of families rather then personalities because most leaders will trust at least some family. 

<div align="center">

"...Status & prestige is a zero sum game..." 
[Kazani Twitter Posts](twitter_kazani.md)
</div>

These families have all the prestige and if they cooperate no other families will ever have prestige. The coalition will rule forever. However these families do not trust each other and are in competition. To gain an upperhand a family will invite a those outside of power into power.  In Kegology terms: An invitation is the act of giving prestige to a family without any in return for service.
